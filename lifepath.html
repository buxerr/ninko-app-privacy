Privacy Policy

Life Path Number Calculator (Android app, package com.buddy.lifepath) · Effective date: 20 October 2026

Life Path Number Calculator ("the app") is a numerology calculator made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the names and dates of birth you enter stay on your device, the app has no account and no server, and it shows no ads.

What you enter

  • Names (birth name and an optional current name) and dates of birth are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive them, and the app never sends them anywhere.
  • Your settings (how Y is counted, theme, daily notification time, whether Pro is unlocked) are stored on your device.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include these files in your own device backup. We cannot access that backup.
  • You can delete a person at any time (Edit › Delete), and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and we run no server. The app does not request the Android Internet permission and does not use an advertising ID.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play on your device whether Pro is owned, to unlock it and to restore it. Google processes purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): only if you turn on the optional daily personal-day notification. It is off by default.
  • Network state: lets Google Play Billing run its own background jobs for the purchase check. The app does not use it to send anything anywhere.
  • Billing: Google Play Billing for the one-time Pro purchase.
  • Wake lock, foreground service, run at startup: used by Android's WorkManager to run the daily notification job and to reschedule it after the phone restarts.

The app does not use your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Entertainment only

Numerology readings are provided for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not advice of any kind.
