REVELA

Privacy policy · Revela: Film Negative Scanner (com.buddy.revela) · Effective 20 October 2026

Revela turns photos of film negatives and slides into positive images on your phone. It is built to work offline and to keep your pictures on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What Revela collects

Nothing. Revela does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Its Android manifest does not include the internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

Your photos

  • Photos you take with the scanner and photos you import are stored in the app's private storage on your device so you can edit them.
  • They leave the app only when you choose to: Save writes a JPEG to your gallery (Pictures/Revela) and Share hands a JPEG to the app you pick.
  • Deleting a frame in Revela removes it from the app. Uninstalling Revela removes everything it stored. Revela's data is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

Permissions

  • Camera, to photograph negatives and slides. It is requested when you open the scanner and you can still import photos without it.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only), to save exported photos to your gallery. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.
  • Photo picker: imports use the system picker, which gives Revela only the photos you select.
  • Google Play billing, for the optional one-time Pro purchase.

Purchases

Revela Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and account information under the Google Privacy Policy. Revela only asks Google Play whether Pro is owned and remembers the answer on your device. The developer receives no payment details.

Children

Revela is a general photography tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone.
