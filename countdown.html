Countdown Live Wallpaper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Countdown Live Wallpaper lets you choose an event date and customize a countdown wallpaper. This policy describes what the app handles and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

  • Your selected event date and time, optional text, display settings, colors, font, and wallpaper preferences.
  • If you choose a background image, the app reads the image you select with Android's photo picker or document picker and stores an app-private copy so the wallpaper can display it.
  • The app does not require an account. It does not collect or upload your event details, text, or selected image to a Ninko server.

These settings and images remain in the app's private storage until you remove them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup and device-transfer settings may include the app's settings and wallpaper files in backups managed by Google. Backups are controlled through your Android and Google account settings. Files you export or back up outside the app can remain after uninstalling.

Network use and advertising

The app does not include analytics or crash-reporting services and does not connect to a Ninko-operated server. The current release (version 18 and later) contains no advertising SDK and does not show ads. Older versions may have displayed third-party ads.

Google Play purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing for the optional Pro one-time purchase and restore flow. Google Play handles checkout and payment credentials under Google's terms and privacy policy. Countdown does not access your payment card or billing credentials and does not send purchase tokens or personal information to a Ninko server. The app receives purchase status from Google Play and stores the Pro entitlement on your device. The price shown in Google Play is the applicable price for your account and region.

Google Play privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.

System features and other apps

Android provides the photo and document picker used when you select an image, and the system wallpaper picker used to apply the wallpaper. Those system features are governed by Google's and your device manufacturer's privacy terms. If you choose to contact support by email, your email address and message are handled by your email provider and received by Ninko to respond.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no account or online community features.

Your choices and deletion

Remove a selected background image or reset the app's settings in the app. You can delete all app-private data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Manage Android backups, transferred copies, and exported files through the relevant Google, Android, or destination-app controls. To request deletion of support correspondence, email nikotfic@gmail.com with “Countdown” in the subject.
