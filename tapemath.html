Tapemath – privacy policy

Feet Inch Fraction Calculator (Android, package com.buddy.tapemath)

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Short version

Tapemath is a calculator. It has no account and no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no server. Your measurements, paper tape and saved projects stay on your phone. The app does not collect or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

  • The numbers you type, the paper tape and saved projects (files inside the app's private storage).
  • Your settings: theme, display units, fraction precision and whether Pro is unlocked.

This data is never sent to the developer. If your phone's Android backup is on, Android may include these files in your own Google backup, which the developer cannot access. Tapping Share or CSV sends a line or a project only to the app you choose. Delete data with Clear on the tape, Delete on a project, or by uninstalling the app.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play, on your phone, whether Pro is owned. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.

Permissions

  • Billing – Google Play's one-time Pro purchase.
  • Network state – declared by Google Play's billing library so that it can tell whether the phone is online when it talks to the Play Store app. The app itself does not ask for internet access and never connects to a server of its own.

The app has no ads and does not use the advertising ID, camera, microphone, location, contacts or your files.

Children

Tapemath is a tool for adults in the building trades and DIY. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
