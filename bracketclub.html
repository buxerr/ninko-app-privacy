Bracket Club — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Bracket Club helps you create knockout and round-robin tournaments, record results, and share a draw from your Android device. This policy explains what the app stores and what happens when you use its optional Google Play purchase and sharing features.

Information stored on your device

Tournament names, team names, fixtures, scores, standings, and the local status of the optional Pro purchase are stored in private app storage on your device. The app does not require an account and does not send tournament information to a Ninko server. Android app backup is disabled. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove this data.

Google Play Billing

Bracket Club uses Google Play Billing to display the localized price of the optional Pro one-time purchase, open checkout, check purchase ownership, and restore a purchase. Google Play handles payment and billing information under Google's terms and privacy policy. Bracket Club receives purchase status and stores the resulting Pro entitlement on your device. The app does not send purchase tokens or payment details to a Ninko server.

Google Play privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

If you choose “Share to your group,” Android opens the system share sheet with tournament information that you choose to share. The app does not send it itself. The receiving app or service handles the information under its own privacy policy.

Collection, advertising, and analytics

Ninko does not collect or receive personal information through Bracket Club. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social networking SDKs. It has no account, location, microphone, camera, or contacts features.

Retention and deletion

Your tournament data and locally stored Pro status remain in the app's private storage until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Google Play purchase records are handled by Google Play and can be managed through your Google account. To ask Ninko to delete support correspondence, email nikotfic@gmail.com with “Bracket Club” in the subject.

Children

Bracket Club does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no account or online community features.
