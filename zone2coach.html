Zone 2 Coach privacy policy

App: Zone 2 Coach (com.buddy.zone2coach) · Effective date: 20 October 2026

In short

Zone 2 Coach works offline. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no internet permission. Your heart-rate data stays on your phone. We (the developer) never receive any of your data.

Health Connect

If you allow it, the app reads two data types from Health Connect. It never writes to Health Connect.

  • Resting heart rate (last 30 days): used once, when you tap "Import from Health Connect", to calculate your Karvonen (heart-rate reserve) zones. Only the resulting number is saved in your settings.
  • Heart rate (last 8 weeks): used only if you switch on "Count Health Connect heart rate" (a Pro feature) to add zone 2 minutes recorded by your watch or other apps to your weekly goal. The samples are processed in memory on your phone and are not stored.

Health Connect data is not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than showing your own zones and weekly totals. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect (Settings → Open Health Connect permissions in the app). The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Bluetooth heart-rate strap

The app scans for and connects to the heart-rate strap you choose. It reads heart rate, sensor contact, RR intervals and battery level. On Android 12 and later, Bluetooth scanning is declared "never for location". On Android 8 to 11, Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scans; the app never reads or stores your location.

What is stored on your phone

  • Settings: age, optional sex, max-heart-rate formula or measured maximum, resting heart rate, zone settings, alert preferences, weekly goal, and the name and Bluetooth address of your strap.
  • A summary of each session: start time, duration, average and maximum heart rate, and minutes in each zone.

This data lives in the app's private storage. It may be included in Android's own device backup if you use it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect), or location on Android 8–11: to find and connect to your strap.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show live heart rate and zone while a session runs with the screen off.
  • Vibration: for zone alerts.
  • Health Connect heart rate and resting heart rate: as described above.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app only learns whether Pro is owned. Payment details are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Zone 2 Coach is not directed at children and is intended for adults.

Not a medical device

Zone 2 Coach is a training aid. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Heart-rate formulas are estimates. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme.
