Courtside Privacy Policy

Courtside: Basketball Scoreboard & Timer (Android, package com.buddy.courtclock)
Effective date: 20 October 2026

Courtside is a basketball scoreboard, shot clock and game timer. It is made by an independent developer (Niko, Croatia). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. In short: your games stay on your devices, there is no account, there are no ads, and the app has no internet permission at all.

What stays on your device

  • The game: scores, fouls, timeouts, clocks, periods, team names and colours, and the rules you choose. It is saved on the device after every change so that a closed app never loses a game.
  • Settings: sound on/off, your rules preset, team names, whether Pro is owned, and a random install id used as the link name.
  • We do not run any server and receive none of this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Android's own backup may include the settings in your personal Google backup; we cannot access it.

Two-device link (Pro)

When you link a second device, the game is sent directly from your phone to your other phone or tablet using Google Nearby Connections over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. It does not use the internet and does not pass through any server of ours. Nearby Connections encrypts the connection. While the devices are pairing, your device name (for example "Pixel 9") and a random install id are visible to nearby devices that are searching for Courtside, and both screens show the same 4-digit code so you can confirm the right device.

Permissions and why

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan, advertise, connect) and nearby Wi-Fi devices: only to find and connect to your other device for the link. Asked only when you open the link.
  • Location (Android 12L and older only): on those Android versions the system requires the location permission for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning. Courtside never reads, stores or shares your location. On Android 13 and newer it is not requested at all.
  • Google Play Billing: for the optional one-time Pro purchase.
  • Network state: lets Google Play Billing and Nearby check whether a network exists. It reads no data. Courtside has no internet permission and no advertising ID permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet itself.

No ads

Courtside shows no advertising of any kind, in the free version or in Pro, and contains no advertising SDK. It does not use your advertising ID.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play on your device whether Pro is owned, so it can unlock it and restore it.

Children

Courtside is made for adults: coaches, referees, scorers and parents. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

No analytics, no selling of data

Courtside contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own and we do not sell or rent any information.
