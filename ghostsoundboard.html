Ghost Soundboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Ghost Soundboard plays sound effects bundled with the app. This policy describes what the app handles and how it is used.

Information handled by the app

  • Your favorites and custom sound names are stored in private app storage on your device so the app can remember your soundboard setup.
  • The app stores a local Pro purchase entitlement so it can remember whether the optional sound-layer upgrade is unlocked. Google Play remains the source for checking and restoring a purchase.
  • The sound effects are included in the app. The app does not record audio, access the microphone, or upload sounds.
  • The app does not require an account and does not collect or send personal information to a Ninko server.

Ghost Soundboard does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. It does not request location or microphone access, and it has no online community or user-upload features. Favorites, custom names, and the cached purchase entitlement stay on your device. Android backup is disabled for this app. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove locally stored app data.

Google Play purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing to show the optional Pro one-time purchase, check purchase status, complete checkout, and restore purchases. Google Play handles checkout and payment credentials under Google's terms and privacy policy. Ghost Soundboard does not access your payment card or billing credentials and does not send purchase tokens or personal information to a Ninko server. The app receives purchase status from Google Play and stores the Pro entitlement on your device. The price shown in Google Play is the applicable price for your account and region.

Google Play privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.

System features and other apps

Android's audio system plays the bundled sounds. If you choose “Rate Us On Google Play,” the app opens the Google Play Store; Google handles any rating or review under its own terms and privacy policy. If you contact support by email, your email address and message are handled by your email provider and received by Ninko to respond.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no account or online community features.

Your choices and deletion

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove locally stored app data. To request deletion of support correspondence, email nikotfic@gmail.com with “Ghost Soundboard” in the subject.
